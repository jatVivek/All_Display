## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, industrial-luxe digital presence for wholesale retail fixtures, boutique store fittings, mannequins, and modular display systems. The interface merges the structural discipline of architectural blueprints with the sensory richness of luxury retail showrooms.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Architectural & Structural:** Deliberate, balanced, and engineered. The UI communicates weight, durability, and commercial-grade structural integrity.
- **Understated Luxury:** Evokes bespoke flagship boutiques through warm travertine stone tones, brushed champagne brass accents, and deep matte industrial slate.
- **Commercial Trust:** Tailored for retail architects, interior designers, visual merchandisers, and apparel chain owners who require precise technical dimensions, load capacities, finish swatches, and frictionless bulk inquiries.

### Design Movement
An intentional synthesis of **Modern Architectural Minimalism** and **Tactile Industrial-Luxe**. Clean geometric structure, refined thin-line borders, expansive warm negative space, and physical finish materiality replace generic flat enterprise tropes.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from high-end retail interior fit-outs: raw cast iron, brushed brass hardware, honed travertine slabs, and oiled natural teak.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1A1C20` - Matte Deep Slate):** Anchors headlines, primary interactive states, structural framing, and high-contrast navigational elements. Supported by `#121316` for maximum contrast surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` - Brushed Champagne Gold):** Reserved for luxury accents, premium badges, active finish selection rings, key callouts, and curated editorial framing. Light variations (`#E5C358`) handle subtle gold tints; deep variations (`#9E7B1A`) ensure AA text compliance on light grounds.
- **Tertiary (`#8C5E3C` - Warm Walnut / Teak Wood):** Used for tactile retail callouts, natural wood material indicators, and warm secondary category tagging.
- **Neutral Ground (`#F4F0EA` / `#FBF9F5` - Travertine Ivory):** Warm, stone-tinted backdrop layers that soften glare compared to raw white, mimicking curated gallery and showroom walls.

### Functional System Colors
- **WhatsApp Direct (`#25D366` / `#128C7E`):** Strictly isolated for commercial quote triggers, direct sales chat, and real-time wholesale catalog inquiries. Never diluted into generic UI iconography.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E5E0D8` / `#D8D1C5`):** Razor-thin dividers and framing borders engineered to preserve architectural structure across dense technical specs.

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances boutique editorial prestige with high-density commercial engineering data.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the legacy craft of haute couture showcases, architectural monographs, and luxury fixture manufacturing. Reserved for section titles, hero statements, and collection names.
- **Technical & Catalog Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean optical geometry, precise legibility inside dense wholesale dimension tables, load ratings, SKU indicators, and tiered volume pricing schedules.
- **Uppercase Labels & Metatags (`label-caps`):** Formatted with deliberate tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic architectural blueprint legends and technical drawings.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly mathematical 4px/8px modular rhythm establishes structural balance across wholesale catalog grids and specification sheets.

### Layout Blueprint
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column responsive layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer canvas margins (minimum 32px to 48px). Product listings adapt cleanly to 3-column and 4-column configurations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters. Filters collapse into a slide-over architectural drawer.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and edge margins. Dense technical spec sheets switch to horizontal swipe tables or single-column stacked accordions.

### Rhythm Rules
- Product detail cards enforce an internal padding of `1.25rem` (20px) to balance technical copy with large high-resolution fixture photography.
- Vertical section spacing strictly alternates between `space-lg` (inside cards and groupings) and `space-xl` (between distinct catalog sections).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient warm lighting, mimicking directional gallery spotlights playing against textured architectural stone.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** Honed Travertine Ivory (`#FBF9F5`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Modules, Input Wells):** Pure Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by fine structural borders (`#E5E0D8`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Quickviews, Sticky Bars):** Elevated Off-White with warm golden ambient occlusion: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 28, 32, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Inquiry Modals, Drawer Cart):** High-depth sheet with ambient deep slate shading: `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.16)`.

### Border Discipline
All structural panels maintain a strict 1px border. Depth is primarily communicated through the interplay of delicate outlines (`#E5E0D8`), subtle background shifts (`#F4F0EA`), and warm low-opacity shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs controlled, architectural corner radiuses that echo milled metal tube fittings, brushed extruded channels, and bevelled timber shelving.

- **Micro Elements (Tags, Swatches, Checkboxes):** 4px to 6px corner radius to maintain precision.
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** 8px corner radius (`rounded-md` base) balancing engineered structure with tactile accessibility.
- **Elevated Modals & Floating Drawers:** 12px to 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`), presenting smooth, welcoming silhouettes.
- **Material Swatches:** True circular pills (full radius) enclosed within an active double-ring architectural bezel.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Architectural Slate):** Solid `#1A1C20` fill, pure white text, 8px radius. Hover state subtly lightens to `#2A2D34` with an ambient warm shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Champagne Gold Accent):** Outlined with 1.5px `#C59B27`, background `#FDFBF7`, text `#1A1C20`. Hover triggers a soft gold tint (`#F9F3E5`).
- **WhatsApp Wholesale Trigger:** Dedicated `#25D366` button with `#FFFFFF` label and WhatsApp icon. Hover translates to dark emerald `#128C7E`. Features explicit context: "Inquire via WhatsApp" or "Get Wholesale B2B Rate".

### Technical Catalog Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 1px `#E5E0D8` border on `#FFFFFF` ground. 
- **Image Canvas:** Dedicated 4:5 vertical framing with subtle `#F4F0EA` backdrop for fixture photography (mannequins, triple pipe racks, wall slots).
- **Material Swatch Row:** Interactive micro-circles (18px diameter) showcasing real fixture materials:
  - Matte Black (`#1A1C20`)
  - Brushed Architectural Gold (`#D4AF37`)
  - Mirror Chrome (`#E0E2E5`)
  - Natural Oiled Teak (`#8C5E3C`)
- **Metadata Badges:** Top-anchored mini pills displaying "MOQ: 5 Units", "Heavy Duty: 120kg", or "Modular Channel".

### Bulk Tiered Pricing Tables
- **Layout:** Monospaced tabular figures with subtle alternating rows (`#FFFFFF` / `#FAF8F4`).
- **Breakdown Columns:** Volume Range (e.g., 1–10, 11–50, 51+ units), Unit Price, and Savings Margin. 
- **Active Selection Tier:** Framed in a 1px brushed gold border (`#C59B27`) with a soft champagne wash.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Selectors
- **Fields:** Clean 1px `#D8D1C5` border, Travertine base `#FFFFFF`, 8px radius. Focus state swaps to 1.5px solid `#1A1C20` with a soft gold focus ring (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Structural square with slightly rounded corners (3px), active state filled with `#1A1C20` and crisp white checkmark.

### Navigation & Wholesale Cart Drawer
- **Sticky Mobile Navigation Bar:** Bottom-docked bar containing Catalog, Finishes, WhatsApp Direct, and Quote Cart triggers. Backed with an ultra-subtle blurred ivory glass (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **B2B Inquiry Modal & Cart Drawer:** High-contrast itemized list with finish swatches, technical dimensions, project room tagger, and single-click "Request Formal Commercial Quotation" action.
