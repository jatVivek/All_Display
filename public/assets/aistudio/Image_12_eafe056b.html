Architect & Contractor Q&A

Frequently Asked Wholesale Setup Questions

Can we customize dimensions for low ceiling basements or mezzanine floors?

Yes. Since our fabrication unit operates directly in Ranigunj, we can laser-cut and notch upright pipe racks to your exact millimetric ceiling clearances (from 6ft to 12ft upright heights).

Do you deliver to Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Maharashtra?

Daily dispatches operate from Hyderabad to Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Bengaluru, Tirupati, Pune, and Chennai via dedicated transport partners with full insurance and tracking.

Will the brushed gold finish peel or tarnish in humid coastal weather?

We utilize genuine PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) titanium coating over 304-grade stainless steel, completely eliminating peeling, salt-air oxidation, and hanger scratching.

How do we verify rack sturdiness before paying?

We arrange an immediate WhatsApp video call from our Ranigunj depot. Our team tests load capacity with 100+ kg live garment loads on camera and inspects crate packaging before loading.
